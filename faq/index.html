Tachanun: frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Tachanun and how this site works.

What is Tachanun?

Tachanun (תחנון) is a prayer of confession and supplication said after the Amidah at Shacharis and Mincha on ordinary weekdays. Its central part is Nefilas Apayim, “falling on the face”. Read more in What is Tachanun?

Why is Tachanun not said on certain days?

Tachanun is a prayer of contrition, so it is left out on days with a festive character, such as Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, Purim and the whole month of Nissan. See all the occasions.

Is Tachanun said at Mincha the day before a holiday?

For many occasions, no: Tachanun is left out at Mincha the afternoon before, for example on Erev Rosh Chodesh, Erev Chanukah, and the afternoon before Lag BaOmer. For others, such as Erev Rosh Hashanah and Pesach Sheni, Mincha the day before has Tachanun as usual. Each holiday page says which.

Is Tachanun said at Maariv?

No. Tachanun is said only at Shacharis and Mincha. Part of the bedtime Shema follows the Tachanun calendar, and the checker shows tonight’s answer for it.

Is Tachanun said on Friday?

At Shacharis on Friday, yes, unless Friday is itself a no-Tachanun day. At Mincha on Friday afternoon, Chabad custom is not to say Tachanun. It is never said on Shabbos.

Is Tachanun said in a house of mourning?

No. Tachanun is not said when davening in a shiva house. Ask your Rav about any particular situation.

Is Tachanun said when a chassan or a bris is in shul?

Tachanun is not said when a chassan during his week of sheva brachos is in the minyan, or when the father, sandek or mohel of a bris taking place that day is present. These depend on who is there, so the checker cannot show them.

How is Chabad custom different?

Chabad omits Tachanun on the Chassidic festivals (Yud Kislev, Yud Tes and Chof Kislev, Yud Beis and Yud Gimmel Tammuz), from 9 Tishrei through Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan, and from Rosh Chodesh Sivan through 12 Sivan. On Gimmel Tammuz, Chai Elul and Hei Teves the public custom is to say Tachanun, and the checker marks these days with an asterisk.

Does the site include Yom Ha’atzmaut or Eretz Yisrael dates?

No. It follows the fixed-date Chabad omissions for the Diaspora, and does not include Yom Ha’atzmaut, Yom Yerushalayim or other Eretz Yisrael-specific days.

When does the answer switch to tomorrow?

The Shacharis and Mincha boxes switch to the next day at nightfall (tzeis hakochavim). The Hebrew date turns over at sunset. The Shema box shows the current night from nightfall until dawn. Times follow Chabad.org’s opinions for your location.

Can I see a whole year at once?

Yes. The Tachanun calendar lists every day in a year with no Tachanun, with English and Hebrew dates. Developers can use the free JSON feed (CC BY-NC 4.0).

Can I install it as an app, or show it in shul?

Yes. On your phone, use “Add to Home Screen” to install it as an app. For a screen in shul, open istheretachanun.com/kiosk for a full-screen portrait display; add ?city=Crown Heights (or any city) to fix the location.
