Why there is no Tachanun on Sukkos

Sukkos is zman simchaseinu, the season of our rejoicing. Tachanun is not said on Yom Tov or Chol Hamoed, on Shemini Atzeres and Simchas Torah, or on Isru Chag, the day after the festival.

Following Chabad custom, Tachanun is not said at all from Erev Yom Kippur (9 Tishrei) through Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan. The first weekday Tachanun after the holidays is on 2 Cheshvan.

The other two festivals work the same way: there is no Tachanun all of Nissan (Pesach) and from Rosh Chodesh Sivan through 12 Sivan (Shavuos).
