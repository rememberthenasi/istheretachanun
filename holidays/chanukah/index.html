Is there Tachanun on Chanukah?

Why there is no Tachanun on Chanukah

Chanukah celebrates the victory of the Chashmona’im and the miracle of the oil. Its eight days, from 25 Kislev, were set aside “for thanks and praise”: full Hallel is said every day and Al HaNissim is added. Tachanun, a prayer of contrition, is not said.

The joy of the festival begins in the afternoon before, so Tachanun is also left out at Mincha on 24 Kislev.

Rosh Chodesh Teves always falls during Chanukah, so on one or two of these days there is also Rosh Chodesh. When Kislev has 30 days, Chanukah ends on 2 Teves; when it has 29, on 3 Teves.
