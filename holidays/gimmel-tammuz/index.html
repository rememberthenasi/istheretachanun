Why the custom varies

On 3 Tammuz 5687 (1927), the death sentence against the previous Rebbe, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn, was commuted to exile, the first step of his liberation, which was completed on Yud Beis–Yud Gimmel Tammuz. Gimmel Tammuz is also the day of the Rebbe’s passing in 5754 (1994).

Tachanun is omitted on days when a special joy is felt. The public custom on 3 Tammuz is generally to say Tachanun, though some individuals may leave it out privately for personal reasons. The checker marks the day with an asterisk for this reason.
