Why the custom varies

Chai Elul, the 18th of Elul, is the birthday of the Baal Shem Tov, the founder of Chassidus (5458 / 1698), and of the Alter Rebbe, the founder of Chabad (5505 / 1745). It is often called the day that brings “life” (chai) into the month of Elul.

Some sources mention leaving out Tachanun on this day, but in the Rebbe’s minyan Tachanun was said. The checker marks the day with an asterisk.
