Is Tachanun said in the month of Nissan?

תחנון · ניסן

No. Tachanun is not said for the entire month of Nissan, from Rosh Chodesh through the end of the month, including Pesach and Isru Chag. It is also left out at Mincha on the afternoon before Rosh Chodesh Nissan.

Why there is no Tachanun on Nissan

On each of the first twelve days of Nissan, when the Mishkan was dedicated, the Nasi (prince) of one tribe brought his offering, and that day was a festival for his tribe. Then come Erev Pesach, the seven days of Pesach and Isru Chag.

Since most of the month is festive, the whole of Nissan is treated as a time of joy (Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chaim 429:2), and Tachanun is not said all month.

Tachanun is first said again on 2 Iyar, since 1 Iyar is itself Rosh Chodesh. Later in Iyar it is also left out on Pesach Sheni and Lag BaOmer.
