Why the custom varies

Hei Teves (5 Teves) marks the victory known as Didan Notzach (“our side has won”). On this day in 5747 (1987) a US federal court ruled that the library of the previous Rebbe belongs to Agudas Chassidei Chabad, and not to any individual.

It is celebrated as a day of joy in Chabad. Even so, the public custom is to say Tachanun, and the checker marks the day with an asterisk to show that individual practice varies.
