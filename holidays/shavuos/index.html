When is Tachanun said again after Shavuos?

תחנון · שבועות

According to Chabad-Lubavitch custom. Dates are calculated by the same rules as the daily checker.

Tachanun is not said from Rosh Chodesh Sivan through 12 Sivan, including Shavuos and Isru Chag. It is said again from 13 Sivan. It is also left out at Mincha on Erev Rosh Chodesh Sivan.

Why there is no Tachanun on Shavuos

The days leading up to Shavuos are days of preparation for receiving the Torah: Rosh Chodesh Sivan, 2 Sivan (Yom HaMeyuchas) and the three days of hagbalah, when the people prepared themselves at Mount Sinai. Shavuos falls on 6–7 Sivan (in the Diaspora) and 8 Sivan is Isru Chag.

In the time of the Beis HaMikdash, the festival offerings of Shavuos could be brought during the seven days after it, through 12 Sivan. Following Chabad custom, Tachanun is not said from Rosh Chodesh Sivan through 12 Sivan, and it returns on 13 Sivan.
